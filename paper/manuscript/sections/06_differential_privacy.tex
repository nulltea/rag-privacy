\section{Differential Privacy}
\label{sec:dp}

% --- scope-framing (short role-framing; bodies stay \needswork) ---
Differential privacy adds calibrated noise to obtain a formal---but
probabilistic---guarantee, applied to embeddings (a noised forward pass), to queries
(distance-DP perturbation), or to the generated output (output-DP against extraction).
Like obfuscation its cost is near-zero compute; unlike obfuscation it buys a provable
bound, paid for in utility as $\varepsilon$ tightens. The output-side variant
(DP-RAG/DP-KSA) is notable for composing orthogonally with any retrieval mechanism,
making it a generic defense against the corpus-extraction attacks of
\cref{sec:synthesis}.
