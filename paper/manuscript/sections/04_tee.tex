% --- scope-framing (short role-framing; bodies stay \needswork) ---
A trusted execution environment runs the computation in plaintext inside a
hardware-isolated enclave, attested to the client over RATLS. Pure-TEE inference is
our \emph{baseline}: it achieves near-plaintext speed and shifts trust to the silicon
vendor, but at full scale it demands either a CPU enclave large enough for the model
or a \emph{confidential GPU} (H100/B200 CC). That hardware requirement---not the
performance---is what motivates the commodity-GPU hybrid split (\cref{sec:hybrid}); we
therefore keep this section to the performance ceiling and the hardware-availability
axis. The strongest adversary in our corpus appears here: Opal~\cite{kaviani_opal}
assumes a \emph{malicious host} and additionally hides access patterns with ORAM.
